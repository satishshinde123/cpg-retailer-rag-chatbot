Retail Return and Refund Policy
Synthetic CPG/Retail dataset for educational RAG projects.
Standard Returns
Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible products.
Products must be unused, unopened and in their original packaging unless the product was received damaged.
A valid invoice or order reference is required for a return request.
Approved refunds are normally processed to the original payment method.
Damaged or Incorrect Products
Customers should report visible damage or an incorrect product within 48 hours of delivery.
Photographs of the package and product may be requested for verification.
If the retailer confirms that the product was damaged in transit or incorrectly supplied, replacement or refund may
be offered.

Retail Store Operations Guide
Synthetic CPG/Retail dataset for educational RAG projects.
Daily Store Process
Opening staff should verify cash procedures, POS availability and critical inventory alerts.
Receiving staff should compare delivered quantities against the purchase order and delivery document.
Damaged cartons should be separated from saleable inventory and recorded in the inventory system.
At closing, store teams should reconcile sales transactions and inventory exceptions.
Merchandising Guidelines
Fast-moving products should be placed in high-visibility areas where practical.
Products with shorter shelf life should follow first-expiry-first-out handling.
Promotional displays should clearly show the offer conditions and validity dates.
